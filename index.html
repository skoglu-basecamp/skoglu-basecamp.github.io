About this log

Welcome to the Skoglu Sightings Log.

This is an independently maintained record of Skoglu sightings as they appear on the Skoglu Observatory. Whenever a new sighting appears, I add it here. I do my best to keep things up to date, though I can't promise every listing will appear the moment it's seen on the website.

I know Skoglu is, at its core, a stuffed animal toy—a designed object with a story. But I like documenting the sightings and imagining Veldtloor Forest as a place that's just out of reach, unexpectedly waiting at the edge of a quiet walk.

This site isn't official. It's my fan project, kept alive by curiosity and a bit of free time.

See you at the trailhead of Veldtloor Forest.
